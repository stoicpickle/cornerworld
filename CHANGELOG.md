# Changelog

Cornerworld follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-02

### Added

- The Overland ambient simulation for macOS.
- Fixed 320×200 SpriteKit presentation and menu-bar controls.
- Deterministic seeded journeys and a terminal runner.
- Weather fronts, terrain, landmarks, river crossings, health, illness,
  supplies, hunting, trade, breakdowns, and trail opportunities.
- Four travel paces and three meaningful ration levels.
- Trail journal, memorial records, and journey summaries.
- Automated simulation regression coverage and release-build validation.
