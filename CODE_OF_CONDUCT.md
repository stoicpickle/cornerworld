# Code of Conduct

Cornerworld should be a welcoming place to build, discuss, and critique small
software worlds.

Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not
harass, demean, threaten, or discriminate against others. Assume good faith,
criticize ideas and code rather than people, and make room for contributors with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or other
contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

To report conduct that should not be handled publicly, use the project's
[confidential report form](https://github.com/stoicpickle/cornerworld/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical.
