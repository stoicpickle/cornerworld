# Historical-content note

Overland is a compact fictional simulation inspired by the constraints and
immediacy of early computer journey games. It is not a curriculum, documentary,
or comprehensive representation of westward migration.

The project avoids treating Indigenous people as a generic danger event.
Region-specific encounters are framed around trade, guidance, travel knowledge,
and established communities. The initial framing was checked against public
National Park Service material:

- [Oregon National Historic Trail history and culture](https://www.nps.gov/oreg/learn/historyculture/index.htm)
- [Making the trek](https://www.nps.gov/oreg/learn/historyculture/making-the-trek.htm)
- [Traveling the Oregon Trail](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/travel-blog-the-oregon-trail.htm)
- [Overlanders in the Columbia River Gorge](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/overlanders-in-the-columbia-river-gorge-1840-1870-a-narrative-history.htm)

These sources do not make every event or line authoritative. Names, regions,
mechanics, and compressed timelines can still be incomplete or mistaken.
Specific, sourced corrections are welcome through issues and pull requests.

Cornerworld is independent and unaffiliated with the creators or rights holders
of *The Oregon Trail*. It includes no copied game code or original game assets.
